Privacy & your inquiry

A clear explanation of how this website handles the details you choose to share.

On this website

Forms prepare a request in your browser. We do not store form entries on this website or use analytics or advertising cookies.

When you send an email

Your email application and email provider process your message. Ashid Apex Consulting receives the details you send to respond to your inquiry and discuss services.

Share only what is needed

Use business contact details where possible. Do not include passport numbers, identity documents, medical records or other sensitive information in an initial inquiry.

Questions and requests

Contact info@aac.mn with questions about your information or a request to correct or delete information you have sent.
