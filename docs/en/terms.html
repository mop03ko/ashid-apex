Information to help you use this website.

General information

Website content describes our recruitment services. It is not an offer of employment, a placement guarantee or a binding service quotation.

Separate agreements

Recruitment scope, fees, responsibilities and employment conditions are agreed separately in writing. Sending an inquiry does not form a service contract.

Documentation and approvals

Requirements and official decisions depend on the circumstances of each assignment. Documentation assistance does not guarantee a visa, permit or approval.

Accuracy and contact

Please contact our team to confirm availability, timelines and the information relevant to your requirements.
