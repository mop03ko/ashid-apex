TRANSPARENCY

A responsible recruitment approach

Clear role requirements, transparent employment conditions and appropriate documentation are central to our approach. Our website does not replace case-specific advice or official decisions. Refer to the relevant authorities for current immigration and employment requirements.

Immigration Agency of Mongolia ↗
What types of workers can you recruit?+

We work with skilled and semi-skilled categories according to employer requirements. Share the roles, skills and experience you need so we can discuss suitability.

How long does recruitment take?+

Timing depends on the position, candidate availability, documentation and applicable requirements. A proposed timeline is discussed after reviewing the requirement.

Who selects the workers?+

The hiring employer makes the final selection following candidate screening and interviews.

Do you work with overseas agencies?+

Yes. We are building long-term B2B relationships with qualified overseas recruitment partners.

Does documentation support guarantee a visa or work permit?+

No. Documentation support is coordination assistance. Decisions remain with the relevant authorities, and requirements must be checked for each case.

How are fees and employment conditions agreed?+

Service scope, fees, responsibilities and employment conditions should be clarified in writing before proceeding. Contact us to discuss your specific requirement.

How do I request workers?+

Complete the employer request form, then review and send the prepared email to our team. You can also call or email us directly.
